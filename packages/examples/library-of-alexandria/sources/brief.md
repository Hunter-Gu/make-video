# Library of Alexandria demo brief

The Library of Alexandria was associated with the Mouseion in Ptolemaic Egypt
and became a symbol of collecting and comparing knowledge.

Its history should not be reduced to a confidently documented single fire that
destroyed one complete collection at one moment. The institutions and
collections associated with Alexandria changed across different periods.

This demo uses an original illustration and cautious explanatory language. It
does not present the illustration as an authentic image of the ancient library.
