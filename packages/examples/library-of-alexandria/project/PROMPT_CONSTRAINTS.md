# Historical prompt constraints

## Period

Ptolemaic Egypt and later ancient Mediterranean periods must be identified per scene; do not blend centuries without disclosure.

## Geography

Use the Mediterranean coast and Alexandria's Hellenistic Egyptian context; avoid generic medieval European settings.

## Clothing

Use period-appropriate linen, wool, drapery, and status markers; avoid modern tailoring.

## Architecture

Use plausible Hellenistic Egyptian civic and scholarly architecture; do not present speculative interiors as documented fact.

## Technology

Show papyrus rolls, ink, reed pens, ships, and ancient construction appropriate to the stated date.

## Prohibited anachronisms

No bound modern books, printing presses, electric light, glass skyscrapers, modern maps, legible invented inscriptions, or medieval costumes.
